SMALL STEPS, EVERYDAY WELLNESS

Nutrition Tips

Build practical habits that make eating well easier.

01

Eat a Variety of Foods

Include fruits, vegetables, grains and protein foods throughout the day.

02

Drink Water Regularly

Keep water nearby and drink regularly, especially in hot weather.

03

Include Fiber-Rich Foods

Choose foods such as beans, vegetables, fruits and whole grains.

04

Enjoy Regular Meals

Choose satisfying meals and snacks that fit your routine and hunger.

05

Cook More Often

Try simple homemade meals and experiment with herbs and spices.

06

Enjoy Your Food

Healthy eating can include your favorite foods. Balance and variety matter.
